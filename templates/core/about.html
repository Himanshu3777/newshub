About NewsHub

NewsHub collects headlines from reputable Indian and international publishers using only their official RSS feeds, cleans and deduplicates them, checks them for sensitive personal data, and watches for claims that started as viral social-media posts.

We do not publish full copyrighted articles and we do not show "verified" tags on regular news. Instead, unconfirmed stories carry a clear source note, and stories found to be fake or misleading are removed from the feed and listed on the Fact Check page. Every story links to its original publisher.
